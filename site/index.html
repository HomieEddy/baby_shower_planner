How it works

Ready the day after you decide

There is nothing to install or host. We set up a private space for your event, and it is yours until the memories are saved.

  1. Pick a plan

    Choose Basic or Premium, then tell us your event date and the web address you'd like.

  2. Get your link

    Within 24 hours you receive your event's link and your private dashboard login by email.

  3. Make it yours

    Name the event, pick colours and a language, add your guests and send the invitations.

  4. Enjoy the day

    Guests check in and find their seats. Afterwards, download the guestbook and photos.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my guests need to download an app or create an account?

No. Guests open their invitation from a link in an email or a text, and on the day they scan a QR code. If they lose the link, a 4-digit code gets them back in.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once for your event, and nothing renews. We don't keep your card on file.

How quickly is my event ready?

Within 24 hours of your payment, you receive an email with your event's link and the login to your dashboard.

Can I start with Basic and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade whenever you like and pay only the difference. A good moment is when the RSVPs are in and it is time to plan the seating and the food.

Is there a limit on the number of guests?

No. Both plans include unlimited guests.

Can my French-speaking guests use it?

Yes. Every page, email and message exists in English and in French. You choose the event's default language and each guest can switch on their own.

What happens to our photos and data after the shower?

You can download the guestbook, the photos and your guest list at any time. About 90 days after the event, the event and all its data are permanently deleted.

Can I use my own web address?

Yes, with Premium. Your event gets a free address on our domain, and we can connect a domain you own instead.

What if I change my mind?

If you haven't used your event yet, write to us within 7 days of your purchase for a full refund.
